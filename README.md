# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

A one-sentence startup pitch for the make.fun bounty [Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a): a startup idea so strange it sounds like a joke, until the business logic lands.

## The pitch

> We stage tiny funerals for dead startups: founders pay $499 to bury their pitch deck in a coffin, and recruiters pay $2,000 a table to meet 200 newly jobless engineers before LinkedIn does.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes the pitch text to `entry.txt` in the directory named by the `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable, or `/output` when it is unset.
